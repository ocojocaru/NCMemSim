## Compatibility and version decision

v1.9.0 is a minor additive release. The public source definitions, explicit exports
and public package aliases of all 140 published v1.8 modules are unchanged.
The independently pinned `v1_8_published_surface.json` was derived from the
published v1.8.0 tag inventory; it cannot be silently regenerated by the new review.
The 297 retained stable paths, 59 stable ensemble exports and 34 multilevel
exports remain protected by the published review/archive gates.

Six additive module-qualified modules expose 20 names: performance contracts,
serial execution/workloads, simulated-data interchange, controlled comparisons
and strict reporting. The existing multilevel reporting optimization is private,
operation-scoped and preserves public signatures, defaults and archive semantics.
No major-version contract break is identified. Performance alone does not justify
v2.0. New APIs and assumptions require this review; they are not automatically
added to the original frozen 297-path stable baseline.

The [v1.9 review](v1_9_api_review.json) captures the current source surface and
normalized source hashes. Validation checks it against source, runtime exports,
the independent published baseline and the strict preparation archive. Updating a
hash does not approve a breaking change or turn a synthetic case into evidence.

## Scientific boundaries

No real TCAD export, proprietary adapter, full co-simulation, independent measured
calibration, new physical coefficient or PDE model is introduced. R3 benefits are
limited to the reviewed same-host restoration/export workloads; no universal
solver speedup is claimed. Native RSS is whole-process peak memory, not solver-only
RAM, and instrumented timings stay separate from plain timings. Interchange
conversions and declared applicability cannot establish external agreement.
Static archives establish consistency, not authentication or replayed dynamics.
