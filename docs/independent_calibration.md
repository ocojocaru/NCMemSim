## Existing software boundaries

`ExperimentalDatasetMetadata`, `OpticalAbsorptionDataset`,
`DeviceObservableDataset` and CSV loaders provide typed values, conditions and
hashes. Metadata has source/DOI/sample/temperature/notes, not a complete
acquisition genealogy or instrument/uncertainty budget. Wavelength uncertainty,
correlated errors and publication/extraction lineage need an explicit P envelope.

`CalibrationCriteria`/`qualify_calibration` distinguish fitting from qualification,
but distinct dataset hashes do not establish different experiments or eliminate
point overlap. Photo qualification rejects a validation hash that is a member
of the training collection; transformed copies/partial overlaps still require
lineage review. Existing photo adapters preserve non-optical protocol settings
and currently fit one capture efficiency; they do not automatically provide
multi-temperature, spectral or structural experimental validation.

Workflow evidence retains source/state/fit/qualification identities and declares
`DataOrigin` as synthetic or measured. A declared origin and successful software
qualification do not independently authenticate a measurement. Existing readers,
status enums and historical regression outputs remain unchanged. Phase P adds an
explicit independent-experimental acceptance envelope; it must not retroactively
promote synthetic or same-curve evidence.

## First scope and data acquisition requirements

First: an opt-in bulk-Ge optical study, on an explicitly reviewed wavelength and
temperature domain using existing optical equations. Select training and external
validation acquisitions before optimization. Begin with a small identifiable
parameter set; freeze bounds, objectives, error treatment and acceptance criteria.
Existing model validity is not expanded merely because a new curve is available.

For each proposed dataset obtain:

- source DOI/version, authors, sample/batch/acquisition IDs, parent curve/file and
  extraction method, exact figure/table/rows, artifact hashes and redistribution terms;
- material/composition, bulk/film/NC distinction, thickness or size/strain metadata,
  temperature and its uncertainty, wavelength/energy units and measurement geometry;
- values, instrument/calibration information, source-reported uncertainties versus
  extraction estimates, detection limits, shared systematic/correlated errors and
  excluded points/reasons; do not invent missing values;
- training/validation groups and complete preprocessing/conversion/digitization
  history. Review acquisition independence, transformed copies, overlapping points
  and shared extraction models/systematics explicitly;
- sufficient domain coverage and observation count for the proposed free parameters.
  Thresholds are dataset-specific, predeclared before fitting/holdout inspection;
  no universal invented RMSE or point-count cutoff is supplied in P0.

A later device/photo study additionally needs named stack geometry/materials,
substrate conditions, NC size/density/active-fraction evidence, initial preparation,
pulse/read/timing/sign conventions, illumination spectrum and irradiance/fluence,
spot/coupling information, matched dark controls and held-out measured conditions.
Constant capture remains a model assumption unless those data qualify it.

## Acceptance and outcomes

Independent experimental qualification requires approved measured-data lineage
plus predeclared numerical/statistical criteria: converged discretization,
identifiability, unchanged parameters on holdout evaluation, uncertainty-aware
residuals, applicability bounds and retained failures. Good training error alone
is insufficient. A shared instrument/model/systematic must be disclosed and may
limit the independence claim even for different specimens.

Missing inputs produce `not_assessable` or an acquisition gap in the proposed
study envelope, not an invented measurement or a model-failure conclusion.
A completed fit remains FITTED unless the explicitly scoped experimental gate
passes. Failed qualifications and negative validation outcomes are deliverables.
No successful calibration or v1.7 release is promised by dataset discovery.

## P1 declared-data admission contracts

`ncmemsim.independent_data` adds five separate contracts: `AcquisitionLineage`,
`UncertaintyBudget`, `ExperimentalDataPackage`, `IndependentStudySplit` and
`AcquisitionGap`. Existing datasets, fit/calibration classes, enums and archive
readers retain their semantics. Package/citation remain published v1.6.0.

```python
from ncmemsim.independent_data import (
    AcquisitionLineage, UncertaintyBudget, ExperimentalDataPackage,
    IndependentStudySplit, AcquisitionGap,
)
```

A data package owns existing `DatasetEvidence` alongside source DOI/locator,
globally scoped study/specimen/acquisition IDs, acquisition roots, observation
parent groups, ordered row identities and ancestor observation-artifact hashes.
IDs must refer to original observations, not just renamed files or derived
parameter tables. Transformations, material/specimen characterization, temperature
and its standard uncertainty, measurement geometry, instrument/calibration,
shared systematic IDs, redistribution terms and reviewer evidence are retained.
Unknown fields remain null/empty with explicit gaps; they are not invented.
Observation-artifact hashes describe numerical observation ancestry, not a shared
paper PDF or archive container containing unrelated experiments.

The uncertainty budget separates reported, estimated, mixed and unknown sources.
It uses standard uncertainties in the canonical dataset units; an expanded error
bar must be explicitly converted and recorded before being supplied as a standard
uncertainty. The legacy uncertainty array is not silently interpreted as one sigma:
its role is standard, converted, absent or unknown. A standard declaration must
match the old array; conversion requires transformation evidence. Independent-axis
uncertainty must be supplied, explicitly reviewed as negligible, or unresolved.
Observable correlations are declared independent, supplied as covariance, or
unresolved. Covariance has squared observable units, matching diagonal standard
uncertainties, symmetry and positive-semidefinite checks with normalized numerical
roundoff tolerance. Singular PSD covariance is retained; eligibility does not
promise invertible whitening or parameter identifiability in a later fit.

A package is `not_assessable` when required source/reviewer/measurement/unit/error
information is missing or unresolved. Synthetic packages are `not_admissible`.
Complete reviewed declarations can be `eligible_for_independent_study`, not
CALIBRATED. This is a source-consistency gate; declared origin, artifact hashes
and reviewer text do not authenticate a laboratory measurement or prove that all
ancestors/uncertainty components have been disclosed. Reviewers remain responsible
for that evidence; thresholds and model fit quality belong to later stages.

The first split policy requires disjoint specimens, acquisitions/roots and
observation ancestry across training and validation. Same dataset hashes/IDs,
shared specimens/acquisitions, parent groups, row IDs or ancestor observation
hashes produce a retained `not_admissible` result even when metadata or transforms
changed the dataset hash. This strict specimen-held-out policy is scoped to the
first independent-study track, not a universal definition of every condition
holdout. Shared systematic IDs require a separate assessment. Even with that
assessment, `statistical_independence_certified` remains false; later objectives
must handle any statistical dependence explicitly. Known overlap takes priority
over missing evidence; gaps alone produce `not_assessable`, not a false model
failure. Neither outcome promotes old fitted/calibrated records.

Archives are immutable, reject unknown schemas/fields, duplicate JSON keys,
nonfinite numbers, unit/source/row-count mismatches and tampered derived summaries.
Restoration rebuilds admission results only; it does not fit, simulate, evaluate
an optical model or replay a random generator.
